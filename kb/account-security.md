# Account and Security

- Account recovery and email ownership changes require identity verification by human support.
- If an account or API key may be compromised, rotate active keys, end unknown sessions, enable multi-factor authentication, and review usage.
- Do not share passwords, verification codes, recovery codes, full keys, or payment details in chat.
- A chatbot must not claim that an account has been recovered, secured, or changed unless a trusted account tool confirms the action.
