# Human Support Escalation

Escalate these cases to an authorized human agent:

- refunds, charge disputes, duplicate payments, or invoice corrections;
- account recovery, ownership changes, or email changes;
- suspected credential compromise or unauthorized usage;
- legal, privacy, or data deletion requests;
- incidents that require access to private production logs.

Before escalation, collect only the minimum safe context: account identifier, time range, request or transaction ID, and a sanitized description. Do not collect secrets.
